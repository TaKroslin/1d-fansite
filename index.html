About this site关于本站

A Mainland China Directioner hub.中国大陆 Directioner 大本营。

FIVE GUYS ONE DIRECTION is an unofficial fan project that preserves the original One Direction visual archive and adds editorial: original essays, curated photo collections, a Mainland China fan-account directory, and a community that lives in the inbox.FIVE GUYS ONE DIRECTION 是一个非官方粉丝项目，保存 One Direction 原始视觉档案并加入编辑内容：原创随笔、精选照片集、中国大陆粉丝账号目录，以及一个"活在邮箱里"的社区。

Everything on this site is free, no login, no tracking. Editorial submissions and account additions go to takionkroslin@icloud.com.本站一切免费，无需登录，无追踪。投稿与账号收录请发送至 takionkroslin@icloud.com。
