FIVE GUYS ONE DIRECTION
About

5GUYS1DIRECTION5GUYS1DIRECTION

A home for One Direction fans in Mainland China.这是专属中国 Directioners 的家。

Built with love, memories, and the hope that great stories should never be forgotten.希望在这里，我们能够寻得爱，回忆，以及永不褪色的好故事。

Why this site?为什么有这个网站?

5GUYS1DIRECTION began with a simple idea:5GUYS1DIRECTION 始于一个简单的念头:

One Direction deserves a place where its music, history, memories, and fans can continue to live together.One Direction 应当在中国拥有一席之地，让他们的音乐、历史、记忆和粉丝能够继续长存。

Over the years, many fan communities disappeared, old websites stopped updating, and countless photos, articles, and memories became scattered across the internet.这些年来,许多粉丝社区消失了,老网站停止了更新，无数照片、文章和记忆散落在互联网各处。

This project brings them together again.这个项目,把它们重新聚到一起。

Not to replace the official website.不是为了取代官方网站。

Not to rewrite history.也不是为了改写历史。

But to continue celebrating it.而是继续为之喝彩。

What you'll find here这里有什么

This website is built around five main ideas.这个网站围绕五个核心板块搭建。

Music音乐 — albums, singles, and the stories behind the songs.——专辑、单曲,以及歌曲背后的故事。

Journal日志 — news, anniversaries, milestones, and moments worth remembering.——新闻、纪念日、里程碑,以及值得记住的瞬间。

Blog博客 — original articles, reviews, and personal reflections written by fans.——由粉丝撰写的原创文章、评论和个人感悟。

Gallery图集 — a growing archive of photographs from every era of One Direction.——覆盖 One Direction 每个年代、不断扩充的照片档案。

This Is Us这就是我们 — a place where One Direction fans in Mainland China can voluntarily share their social accounts and find one another again.——中国粉丝社交媒体收集墙。

Our philosophy我们的理念

This project follows one simple principle:这个项目只遵循一个简单的原则:

Continue, don't redesign.延续那份感受。

Every page, every animation, every typeface, and every interaction is created with respect for the original One Direction website.每一页、每一个动画、每一种字体、每一次交互,都以原版 One Direction 网站为蓝本。

The goal has never been to create something different.我们从来不是为了做一个标新立异的网站。

The goal is to create something that feels like it has always belonged here.而是做出一个一见如故的"老房子"。

For Directioners致所有 Directioner

Whether you've been here since 2010...无论你是从 2010 年就在这里的老粉……

or discovered One Direction yesterday...还是昨天才认识 One Direction……

you're welcome here.我们都欢迎你。

This website belongs to everyone who still smiles when they hear those five voices together.这个网站属于每一个看见五个小伙在一起、还会忍不住笑出来的人。

A personal note作者的碎碎念

I'm a newer Directioner.我不算特别的老粉。

I wasn't there when the albums were first released or when the tours filled stadiums around the world.我没有经历过燥候专辑发行时的激动,也没有亲眼见过他们的巡演点亮一座一座城市。

I met One Direction years later.我是很多年后才遇见 One Direction 的。

But sometimes, loving something isn't about arriving first.但有时候,爱一件事,不是看谁先到。

It's about choosing to stay.而是看你愿不愿意留下来。

This website is my way of saying thank you—to the band, to this community, and to everyone who keeps these memories alive.这个网站,是我说的一声"谢谢"——谢谢这支乐队,谢谢这个圈子,也谢谢所有让这些记忆继续活着的人。

I hope that one day, someone will discover a song here, revisit a forgotten memory, or simply realize they're not the only Directioner still listening.我希望有天,有人会在这里重新听到一首歌、想起一段被遗忘的记忆,或者只是忽然意识到,自己不是唯一一个还在听 One Direction 的人。

If that happens, then this project has already become everything I hoped it would be.如果那一天真的来了,这个项目就已经实现了它的价值。

We Are 1D Family!我们都是 1D FAMILY !
