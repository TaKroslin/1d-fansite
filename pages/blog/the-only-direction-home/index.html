The Only Direction HomeThe Only Direction Home

Five boys, five different roads, one long way home. A serialised novel set in the One Direction universe, written out of love — in Chinese, from the prologue to the last chapter.五个男孩，五条不同的路，一条漫长的回家路。一部 One Direction 宇宙背景的连载小说，因为热爱而写成——中文原稿，从前言写到最后一章。

110 chapters · ~850,000 Chinese characters · complete.110 章 · 约 85 万字 · 中文首发 · 已完载。
