Why I Love 1D So Bad

I was born only one year before One Direction began.我出生的那一年,比 One Direction 成立还早一年。

By the time I truly discovered them, the world had already changed. The tours were over. The hiatus had long begun. The moments everyone talked about had already become history.等我真正认识他们的时候,世界已经变了。巡演结束,”休团”已经持续了很久,所有人谈论的那些瞬间,都已经成了历史。

I wasn’t there when Up All Night was released.《Up All Night》发行的时候,我不在场。

I wasn’t standing in the crowd during the Where We Are Tour.Where We Are 巡演的现场,我也没能站进去。

I never got the chance to hear five voices sing together on the same stage.我从来没有机会,听到五把声音一起在同一个舞台上响起。

In many people’s eyes, I was simply… too late.在很多人眼里,我只是……来得太晚。

But somehow, none of that stopped me from falling in love with them.但不知道为什么,这一切都没能阻止我爱上他们。

The more I watched, the more I realized I wasn’t only listening to music.看得越多,我就越意识到,我听的其实不只音乐。

I was watching five young people grow up together.我看的是,五个年轻人一起长大。

I saw friendships that felt genuine, laughter that couldn’t be scripted, and countless ordinary moments that somehow became unforgettable. Their songs were incredible, but it was the people behind them that kept bringing me back.我看到了真实的友情、没法被剧本写出来的笑、以及无数个不知怎么就变得难忘的日常瞬间。他们的歌很棒,但真正让我一次次回来的,是站在歌后面的那几个人。

Over time, One Direction quietly became part of my own life.慢慢地,One Direction 安静地走进了我自己的生命。

During long nights after school.放学后的一个个长夜。

Between endless homework and exams.做不完的作业和考试之间。

While learning to code.学写代码的时候。

While making films.拍片子的时候。

While dreaming about the future.梦想未来的那些时候。

Whenever life felt overwhelming, I always found myself returning to the same interviews, the same concerts, the same albums, and somehow they never felt old.每次生活压得人喘不过气,我总会回到那些同一些采访、同一些演唱会、同一些专辑——不知道为什么,它们从来不会变旧。

They reminded me that growing up doesn’t mean leaving behind the things that once made you happy.它们提醒我,长大,并不意味着要丢掉那些曾经让你开心的事。

As I got older, I also began to notice something else.长大一些之后,我又开始注意到另一件事。

Many of the places where fans once gathered slowly disappeared. Websites stopped updating. Communities became quieter. Photos vanished. Links broke. Memories scattered across the internet.很多曾经聚集过粉丝的地方,正在一个一个消失。网站停止更新,社群越来越安静,照片消失,链接失效,记忆散落一地。

For fans in Mainland China, it often felt even harder.对中国粉丝来说,这种感受常常更强烈。

Finding reliable information wasn’t easy.找到靠谱的信息,不容易。

Meeting other fans wasn’t easy.见到其他粉丝,不容易。

Feeling connected wasn’t easy.感到自己仍然和这个圈子连在一起,不容易。

That’s one of the reasons I started building 5GUYS1DIRECTION.这也是我开始搭建 5GUYS1DIRECTION 的原因之一。

Not because I wanted to replace the official website.不是因为我想要取代官方网站。

Not because I wanted to build the biggest fan community.不是因为我想要建一个最大的粉丝社区。

Simply because I wanted a place that felt like home.只是因为我想有一个像家一样的地方。

A place where people could read, remember, discover, and maybe even find someone who understands exactly why a ten-year-old song can still mean everything.一个可以读东西、找回回忆、发现新东西、甚至可能遇见一个”完全理解为什么一首十年前的歌今天仍然意义非凡”的人的地方。

People often ask me why I care so much about One Direction.经常有人问我,为什么对 One Direction 这么上心。

The answer has never really changed.答案其实一直没变过。

Because One Direction taught me something that had nothing to do with fame.因为 One Direction 教给我了一些和名声毫无关系的东西。

They taught me how powerful genuine friendship can be.他们让我看到,真正的友谊可以有多大的力量。

They showed me that the best memories are often the ones that were never planned.他们让我看到,最好的回忆,常常是那些从来没被安排过的。

And they gave me something surprisingly rare on the internet—a feeling of belonging.他们还给了我一件在互联网上其实挺稀有的东西——一种”属于这里”的感觉。

Maybe that’s why I love One Direction so bad.也许这就是为什么我这么喜欢 One Direction。

Not because I lived through their biggest years.不是因为他们最火的那几年我正好在场。

But because they became part of mine.而是因为他们后来成了我自己故事的一部分。

If this website helps even one person rediscover a forgotten song, relive an old memory, or meet another fan who makes them feel a little less alone, then everything I’ve built here will have been worth it.如果这个网站能帮哪怕一个人,重新找到一首被遗忘的歌,重温一段旧的回忆,或者遇到另一个让自己不再那么孤单的粉丝——那我所做的一切,就都值得了。

This website is my way of saying thank you.这个网站,是我说谢谢的方式。

Thank you for the music.谢谢你们的音乐。

Thank you for the memories.谢谢那些回忆。

And thank you for helping me become the person I am today.也谢谢你们,让我成为今天的我。
