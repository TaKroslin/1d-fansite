There are already countless places to find One Direction online.

You can stream the music, watch old interviews, scroll through fan edits, or search for photos from almost any era. Information has never been difficult to find.

But over the years, something else has slowly become harder to find.

A place that feels like home.

This site wasn’t created to replace the official website, nor to become another social platform. It exists because many of the memories surrounding One Direction are now scattered across different corners of the internet. Some links have disappeared, some communities have faded away, and some stories are becoming increasingly difficult to revisit.

5GUYS1DIRECTION is an attempt to bring those memories together again.

Here you’ll find official history, fan-written articles, photographs, timelines, and most importantly, people. The “This Is Us” page exists for one simple reason: to help One Direction fans in Mainland China find one another again. Whether you’ve been here since 2010 or only discovered the band recently, you’re part of the same story.

This project will continue to grow over time. New articles will be written. New photos will be archived. New fans will join. Old memories will be rediscovered.

Nothing here is meant to compete with the official website.

Instead, this site hopes to continue where it naturally paused.

One Direction has always been more than five people standing on a stage.

It has always been about the people who found each other because of them.

If this website helps even one fan reconnect with those memories—or with another fan—then it has already fulfilled its purpose.

Welcome home.
