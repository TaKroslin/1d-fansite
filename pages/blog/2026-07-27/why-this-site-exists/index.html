Why This Site Exists

There are already countless places to find One Direction online.网上能找到 One Direction 的地方,已经太多了。

You can stream the music, watch old interviews, scroll through fan edits, or search for photos from almost any era. Information has never been difficult to find.你可以在线听歌,看老的采访视频,翻粉丝剪辑,或者搜到几乎任何时期的照片。信息,从来都不难找。

But over the years, something else has slowly become harder to find.但这些年,另一种东西却越来越难找到。

A place that feels like home.一个让人感觉像家的地方。

This site wasn’t created to replace the official website, nor to become another social platform. It exists because many of the memories surrounding One Direction are now scattered across different corners of the internet. Some links have disappeared, some communities have faded away, and some stories are becoming increasingly difficult to revisit.这个网站不是为了取代官方网站,也不是为了再造一个社交平台。它存在的原因很简单——关于 One Direction 的很多记忆,已经散落在互联网的各个角落。有些链接失效了,有些社区消失了,有些故事越来越难再被翻出来。

5GUYS1DIRECTION is an attempt to bring those memories together again.5GUYS1DIRECTION,是一次把它们重新聚到一起的尝试。

Here you’ll find official history, fan-written articles, photographs, timelines, and most importantly, people. The “This Is Us” page exists for one simple reason: to help One Direction fans in Mainland China find one another again. Whether you’ve been here since 2010 or only discovered the band recently, you’re part of the same story.这里有官方历史,有粉丝写的文章,有照片,有年表——最重要的,是人。”This Is Us”这个页面,只为了一个简单的目的:让中国 One Direction 的粉丝重新找到彼此。无论你是从 2010 年就在这里的老粉,还是刚刚才认识这支乐队,你都是同一个故事的一部分。

This project will continue to grow over time. New articles will be written. New photos will be archived. New fans will join. Old memories will be rediscovered.这个项目会持续生长。新的文章会被写出来,新的照片会被归档,新的粉丝会加入,旧的记忆会被重新翻起。

Nothing here is meant to compete with the official website.这里没有想要和官方网站竞争的意思。

Instead, this site hopes to continue where it naturally paused.只是希望,把当年自然停下来的地方,继续接下去。

One Direction has always been more than five people standing on a stage.One Direction 一直都不只是五个人站在舞台上。

It has always been about the people who found each other because of them.它一直是那些因为他们而遇见的彼此。

If this website helps even one fan reconnect with those memories—or with another fan—then it has already fulfilled its purpose.如果这个网站,能帮哪怕一个粉丝重新找回一段记忆,或者重新联系上另一个粉丝——那它已经完成了它该完成的事。

Welcome home.欢迎回家。
