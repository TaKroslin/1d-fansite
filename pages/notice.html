Notice

A notice about this site关于这个网站的一条公告

Sadly, and with a great deal of calm, I have to announce that很遗憾，也很平静地宣布：

5guys1direction will stop updating officially from 1 October 2026.5guys1direction 将于 2026 年 10 月 1 日起正式停止更新。

What this site was to me这个网站对我意味着什么

This site has been with me for a long time.这个网站陪伴了我一段很长的时间。

From the first idea and the design, to refining the pages and taking care of the content, I put a great deal of time and effort into it.从最初的构思、设计，到后来不断完善页面、整理内容，我投入了很多时间和精力。

It recorded how much I love One Direction. It also recorded everything I have done as a creator along the way.它记录过我对 One Direction 的喜欢，也记录了我作为一个创作者，在这段时间里做过的很多事情。

Why the plan changed计划为什么变了

My original plan was to pause updates after 28 September and bring the site back in July 2027.原本我的计划是 9 月 28 日之后暂时停更，并在 2027 年 7 月重新运营。

But after some things that have happened recently, and after thinking about this experience all over again, I have finally decided:但经过最近发生的一些事情，以及我对这段经历的重新思考，我最终决定：

the official site will not be coming back.不再恢复网站的官方运营。

The code will be open-sourced代码将正式开源

But I do not want this project to end completely just because I am leaving.不过，我并不希望这个项目因为我的离开而彻底结束。

So from 1 October, I will release the source code of 5guys1direction under the MIT License — it is on GitHub at github.com/TaKroslin/1d-fansite.因此，从 10 月 1 日开始，我会将 5guys1direction 的网站源代码正式开源，并采用 MIT License —— 代码托管在 GitHub：github.com/TaKroslin/1d-fansite。

This means the project no longer belongs to one single person.这意味着，这个项目不再属于某一个人。

Anyone is free to use, copy, modify, redesign and build on it within what the license allows — and to run their own version on top of the existing code. Take it as the starting point for your own fan project, port it to another language, or just read the CSS to see how the stacking letters work.任何人都可以在许可证允许的范围内自由使用、复制、修改、重新设计、二次开发，甚至基于现有代码建立属于自己的版本：把它当作自己粉丝项目的起点、翻译成另一种语言，或者只是读读 CSS，看看"叠纸翻页"是怎么实现的。

You can use it你都可以用起来

If you like the design of this site and want the code to build your own version.如果你喜欢这个网站的设计，想拿代码自己改。

If you want to keep maintaining One Direction content.如果你想继续维护 One Direction 的相关内容。

If you want to turn it into your own personal site, a fan site, or something else entirely.如果你想把它改造成自己的个人网站、粉丝站或者其他项目。

You can just start from the open-sourced code.都可以直接从开源代码开始。

Please do not be afraid to change it just because it is "the original site's code", and please do not worry that I will one day come back and ask you to stop using it.不用因为“这是原网站的代码”而不敢修改，也不用担心我之后会突然回来要求你停止使用。

I hope it can really become an open-source project that others can keep using and keep creating with, instead of disappearing together with my decision to stop updating.我希望它真正成为一个可以被继续使用和创造的开源项目，而不是随着我的停更一起消失。

A note on copyright关于版权的说明

Of course, the MIT License mainly covers the source code I publish myself.当然，MIT License 主要针对的是我所发布的源代码本身。

Some of the images, texts, fan works and other third-party content on this site are not created by me and are not owned by me. They still have to follow the copyright and licensing rules that originally apply to them.网站中的部分图片、文字、同人作品以及其他第三方内容，如果并非由我创作或拥有版权，则仍然需要遵守它们原本适用的版权和授权规则。

What happens to the site网站未来会怎样

Whether the site keeps existing is up to whoever comes next.至于网站未来会不会继续存在，就交给后来的人决定。

If someone wants to take it over, it can keep running.如果有人愿意接手，它可以继续运行下去。

If nobody does, then when the current domain expires I will not renew it, and the site will eventually go offline on its own.如果没有人接手，那么等现有域名到期后，我将不再续费，网站最终会自然下线。

Thank you感谢

Thank you to everyone who has ever visited 5guys1direction, to everyone who contributed content, to everyone who left suggestions, and to everyone who simply happened to open this site.感谢所有曾经访问过 5guys1direction、为网站提供过内容、提出过建议，或者只是偶然打开过这个网站的人。

This project was once a part of my life.这个项目曾经属于我的一段经历。

Now I am handing its code to everyone who wants to keep creating.现在，我把它的代码交给所有愿意继续创造的人。

5guys1direction stops updating officially.5guys1direction，停止官方更新。

But the code belongs to the open-source community from today on.但代码，从今天开始属于开源社区。

— Takion—— Takion
