Perfect is OUT NOW

Brand. New. Music! One Direction has released the album track Perfect. Have you heard it yet? Following the release of Drag Me Down and Infinity, Perfect is the band’s third track from the upcoming album Made in the A.M. You can download your copy or listen to it on Spotify now.

In other 1D news, there's still time to take part in the band’s exclusive #1DLondonSession in partnership with Apple Music. The event takes place on November 14, 2015 at a secret location in London and will see the guys perform a special show and interview for fans who have supported them over the past five years.
