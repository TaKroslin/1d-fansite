Action 1D

Action/1D has launched and the guys need your help. One Direction has teamed up with action/2015 to help bring about global change and make a difference in tackling some of the world’s most pressing issues.

Would you like to see a world without poverty? Or stronger efforts to tackle climate change? Whatever your vision, the guys want to know.

Show the world you care by telling everyone about the things that matter to you.

Over the next few months, the band will lead the initiative to get your voices heard on a global scale ahead of world leaders meeting at two key summits.

Want to join the action/1D movement? Find out how you can get involved
