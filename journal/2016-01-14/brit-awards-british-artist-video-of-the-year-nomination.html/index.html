Brit Awards’ British Artist Video of the Year Nomination

Great news 1D Family, the band have been nominated for the Brit Awards 2016’s British Artist Video of the Year award for Drag Me Down. 
The award is a social vote, so the guys will need all of your help to make sure that they take home the prestigious prize.

 
Want to help them win? Here’s how:

 
Votes placed on Twitter using #BRITDRAGMEDOWN will
be counted.

 
You can ONLY vote for the band to win once in a 24-hour period, any duplicate votes won’t be counted.

 
Retweets DON’T count towards the final vote.

 
Only tweets from public Twitter accounts will be
counted.

 
If a tweet is deleted before the voting period ends, it will still be counted!

 
Let’s get voting!
