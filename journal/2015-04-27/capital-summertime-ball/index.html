Capital Summertime Ball.

Yes, it’s true! One Direction are set to make their official debut at Capital FM’s annual Summertime Ball with Vodafone in London on June 6, 2015.

Earlier today (April 27), Liam called into the UK radio station’s breakfast show to confirm the news, stating: “Wembley is obviously one of the best places to play in the world, so I’m well excited!”

After teasing fans on social media, the band were the first act to be unveiled in what the radio station promises to be their most ‘insane’ line-up ever.

Want to see the boys perform live at this year’s show? Keep up-to-date by following the hashtag #CapitalSTB.
