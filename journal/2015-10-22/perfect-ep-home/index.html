Perfect EP + Home!

In less than a week, the guys have given us Perfect, the Perfect music video and now the Perfect EP. The four track
EP features an acoustic version of the guys’ latest single Perfect, as well as a remix by Matoma, the BRAND NEW & exclusive track Home AND the Drag Me Down remix ft LunchMoney Lewis.
